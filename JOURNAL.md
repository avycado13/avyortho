# September 14: Make Schematic and Design
I have had the idea of a 40% ortho floating around in my head for a few days now and with half life prep week one starting today, I thought this would be perfect. I made a design for it in [Keyboard Layout Editor NG](https://editor.keyboard-tools.xyz/)
and then made a quick schematic in KiCad. I used the [Stasis split keyboard guide](https://stasis.hackclub.com/starter-projects/split-keyboard) as a reference for parts of it.
